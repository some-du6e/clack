A native window, not a web page

Clack draws every pixel itself, on your GPU. There's no Chromium, Electron or web view inside, so there's no page to load before your messages show up.

Features

Everything where you left it

Clack copies the 2026 Slack desktop app's layout, so the sidebar, tabs, threads and menus are where you'd look. It's an alpha. Where a screen isn't built yet, Clack hides its menu item instead of faking it.

Channels, with everything in them

Grouped messages, reactions, files and link previews, with canvases, lists, pins and bookmarks in the tabs along the top. A live huddle shows in the channel and in the sidebar.

Every screenshot in the README →

Threads open next to the conversation

A thread opens in a pane on the right with its own message box, so the channel stays in view. Tick "Also send to" and the reply goes to the channel too.

One list for everything that needs you

Mentions, thread replies, reactions, DMs and invites collect in Activity, with All, DMs, Mentions and Threads tabs. Pick an item and its conversation opens beside the list.

Plugins

Bring your Taut and Slick plugins

Taut and Slick are client mods for Slack. Clack builds their plugins unmodified from the upstream source and runs them in a sandboxed JavaScript engine, turning their React components into native widgets.

Themes

Every Slack theme, light or dark

All 28 of Slack's theme presets, custom colours and dark mode. The theme you pick is saved to your Slack account, the way Slack saves it.

  • 28 presets

    Aubergine, Clementine, Banana, Jade, Lagoon, Barbra, Gray, Mood Indigo and the rest, drawn from Slack's own palettes.

  • Your own colours

    Set a custom theme in Preferences, under Appearance, the same way you would in Slack.

  • Light, dark or your system's

    Dark mode can follow your system's setting, as Slack's does.

Open source

Free, open and built by CI

Clack is MIT licensed and built in the open. Every release comes out of CI with a SHA256SUMS file for its downloads.

  • Try it without an account

    Run Clack with demo and it opens Quillfeather Paper Co., a made-up company whose coworkers post, reply, react and start huddles. The demo runs its own fake Slack server, and Clack refuses every connection that isn't to your own computer.

  • It updates itself

    On all three systems Clack downloads each new release, checks it against the release's SHA256SUMS and applies it when you restart.

  • Its own sign-in

    You sign in through your browser, the way the Slack app does, and Clack gets a session of its own. Nothing is copied out of your browser.

    How signing in works →
# Linux, AppImage
$ ./Clack-{{release.version}}-x86_64.AppImage demo
# Linux, from the .deb or .rpm
$ clack demo
# macOS
$ /Applications/Clack.app/Contents/MacOS/clack demo
# Windows, in PowerShell
> cd $env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\Clack
> .\Clack.exe demo
